# FlowSync — 1-page product analytics brief

**Scenario:** Fictional B2B workflow automation SaaS, data covering September 2025–August 2026. Leadership sees new accounts but needs to understand conversion and recurring revenue quality.

**Stakeholders:** Head of Product (onboarding/feature priorities), Growth Lead (channel quality), Customer Success Lead (accounts needing attention), Finance (MRR retention), AI Product Manager (AI feature economics).

**Decision questions:** Where do accounts abandon onboarding? Which acquisition sources convert to paid? What MRR comes from which plans? Which issues touch high-value accounts? Which active customers might need proactive attention? Is an integration-first onboarding change associated with higher short-term activation? What does the AI Assistant cost per adopting account?

**Scope:** Nine interconnected synthetic raw tables, reproducible SQL and Python marts, a functioning 3-tab interactive dashboard, one illustrative experiment, and an optional LLM support-ticket classifier with review safeguards.

**Success criteria:** Reconciled month-end revenue; defensible stage definitions; visible denominator and cohort windows; distinct-account revenue exposure instead of ticket-count multiplication; experiment uncertainty; a clear evidence-led recommendation document.

**Out of scope:** Production integrations, actual customers, trained churn probability, unvalidated causal claims, automated account interventions, cloud data engineering, full enterprise transformation, a finished PBIX, and claimed LLM quality/savings without evaluation.
